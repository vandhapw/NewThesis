Modern smart manufacturing integrates cyber-physical systems, the industrial Internet of Things, and artificial intelligence to create autonomous production environments where unplanned downtime carries severe economic consequences. Predictive maintenance research nonetheless remains fragmented: anomaly detection, remaining useful life estimation, and maintenance scheduling are typically treated as isolated problems; static models degrade under sensor drift; and combinatorial scheduling complexity grows rapidly with fleet size. This thesis proposes the Quantum-Agentic Smart Manufacturing for Anomaly Detection and Predictive Maintenance (QASAMAP) framework, a three-layer real-time architecture that closes the loop from raw Kafka sensor streams to constraint-satisfying maintenance schedules within a single $60$-second cycle.

\textbf{Layer~1 --- Spatio-temporal forecasting and supervised learning.} On a $100{,}000$-reading $50$-machine dataset, a Temporal Graph Convolutional Network with cross-correlation lag-graph construction ($50$ nodes, $538$ directed edges) achieves average $R^2 = 0.7702$ across five sensor channels, outperforming a tuned LSTM ($+12.4\%$) and a hybrid LSTM--GCN ($+8.0\%$). A six-target supervised benchmark across XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, HistGradientBoosting, and TabNet establishes reference performance for anomaly detection, failure-type classification, downtime-risk and maintenance prediction, machine status, and remaining-useful-life regression.

\textbf{Layer~2 --- Four-tier multi-agent orchestrator.} The Kafka-driven cognitive layer buffers per-machine windows of $W=10$ readings and dispatches them through nine specialised agents (Monitoring, Correlation, Pattern Learning, Diagnosis, Planning, Action, Self-Reflection, Explainability, Reporting) coordinated by a phased orchestrator. Heterogeneous LLM routing (GLM-5.1, Kimi-K2.6, DeepSeek-v4-Pro) matches each agent to a task-appropriate model. Intra-phase parallelism reduces worst-case latency from $9 T_{\text{LLM}}$ to $\sim 4 T_{\text{LLM}}^{\max}$. End-to-end validation on $50$ streamed machines forwards $45/50$ to the scheduler at CRITICAL priority and returns a feasible schedule in $36.9$~ms, well inside the Kafka cycle.

\textbf{Layer~3 --- Constraint-aware scheduling.} The scheduling problem is formulated as both MILP and QUBO and benchmarked across MILP/CBC, the Quantum-Inspired Evolutionary Algorithm (QIEA), Simulated Quantum Annealing (SQA), feasibility-aware Simulated Annealing/Genetic Algorithm/Tabu Search, and QAOA with XY ring mixer in both simulation and on IBM Heron~r2 hardware. A fair multi-seed scaling benchmark shows MILP/CBC dominates the basic-constraint regime up to $N=150$ in sub-second time. A complex-constraint streaming experiment enforcing one-hot, slot-window capacity over the $d=4$-h duration, $\rho$-slot risk horizon, and must-schedule rule reveals three measured regimes on IBM \texttt{ibm\_fez}: at $N=3$ ($12$ qubits, depth $801$, $644$ CZ) QAOA-XY post-selects the MILP optimum exactly (cost $300.00$, $10.96\%$ feasibility); at $N=5$ ($20$ qubits, depth $1{,}226$, $1{,}935$ CZ) the problem is structurally infeasible for every classical solver (MILP, QIEA, SA, GA, TS) and QAOA-XY is the only method returning a one-hot- and capacity-feasible schedule (cost $1{,}186.02$, $0.24\%$ feasibility, $+1.3\%$ over MILP best-effort); at $N=7$ ($28$ qubits, depth $3{,}120$, $4{,}063$ CZ) QAOA-XY beats every classical heuristic (cost $3{,}069.07$ with $V=2$ violations vs $V=8$ for the best classical at cost $4{,}295.42$). At $N=9$ ($32$ qubits, $5{,}390$ CZ) the empirical noise frontier is reached: $0\%$ feasibility on \texttt{ibm\_fez}, \texttt{ibm\_kingston}, and the parallel \texttt{ibm\_marrakesh} attempt confirming that the limit on Heron~r2 at $p=2$ is set by gate fidelity and circuit depth, not qubit count.

The contribution of QASAMAP is therefore (i) an honest, reproducible benchmark across spatio-temporal forecasting, multi-agent cognitive reasoning, and constraint-aware scheduling on a 60-s real-time Kafka budget, and (ii) the empirical identification of a constraint-native quantum-advantage regime --- streaming-derived constraint-tight scheduling at $N=3$--$7$ where exact MILP fails or every classical heuristic violates risk-horizon constraints --- in which the XY-ring-mixer QAOA on current NISQ hardware provides measured operational value over the best classical alternatives.
